# Clay prompts for Prmpt.in

## Prompt 1 — Brand analysis
Analyze this D2C brand from its website and public information. Identify what the brand sells, who the target customer is, what emotional or functional benefit the product offers, and what kind of ad creative would likely work for Meta/Instagram. Keep the analysis short, practical, and useful for a creative agency selling AI-powered ad creatives.

## Prompt 2 — Creative weakness
Based on the brand website, Instagram, and available ad examples, identify the biggest creative weakness in their current marketing. Focus on issues like weak hook, repetitive product shots, lack of UGC, lack of founder/storytelling, unclear offer, poor first 2 seconds, weak product demonstration, or no emotional angle. Write this as one sharp observation.

## Prompt 3 — Prmpt.in outreach angle
Create one personalized outreach angle for Prmpt.in, an AI ad and content production studio that helps Indian D2C brands create more UGC-style and cinematic video ads without repeated expensive shoots. The angle should be specific to this brand and should not sound generic.

## Prompt 4 — Hook ideas
Write 3 short Meta ad hooks this brand should test. The hooks should be specific, scroll-stopping, and suitable for a 15-second UGC or cinematic product ad. Keep them natural, not clickbait.

## Prompt 5 — First email line
Write one personalized first line for a cold email to the founder or marketing head of this brand. Mention one specific thing about their product, category, or current ad direction. Keep it human, simple, and under 22 words.
